> Resight exists because people lose track of where their time actually goes once they open a browser. It turns invisible browsing habits into a clear, local, privacy-respecting picture — how long you spend per site, per day, per app — and gives you direct tools (blocking, time limits, content filtering) to act on that picture instead of just observing it.

**Resight is a Chrome extension.** It tracks how much time you spend on websites, blocks distracting sites, enforces time limits, and shows detailed insights into your browsing, YouTube, and AI chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) usage. Resight stores this data locally on your device by default, with no account required.

Resight is privacy-first and works through Chrome's Side Panel, giving people a clear picture of their daily browsing habits along with the tools to act on it — blocking, time limits, and content filtering — without sending data to the cloud unless they choose to.

If you're searching for a **screen time tracker for Chrome**, a **website blocker with time limits**, a **browsing analytics dashboard**, or a tool to **track AI chatbot usage (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)**, Resight is built to cover all of these use cases in one extension.

## What Problem Does Resight Solve?

Most people don't have a clear picture of where their time actually goes online. Browser history shows *what* was visited, but not *how long*, *how often*, or *how it fits into daily habits*. Resight closes that gap by turning raw browsing activity into readable insights, and by giving users tools (blocking, time limits, content filtering) to act on what they learn.

## Who Is It For?

- People trying to reduce doomscrolling and short-form video consumption (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels).
- Students and remote workers who want to measure focused work time versus distraction time.
- Anyone curious about their AI chatbot usage patterns across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Meta AI, and Grok.
- Users who want a local-first, privacy-respecting alternative to cloud-only screen time apps.

## Core Features

### Browsing Activity Tracking
Tracks the currently active site, live and total time spent per day, percentage of activity per site, and a ranked "top websites" list. Distinguishes actively-used tabs from background tabs using real user interaction signals (mouse, keyboard, scroll).

### Insights & Historical Trends
Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly summaries with trend charts, activity heatmaps, and hour-by-hour breakdowns of browsing behavior.

### Activity Board & Sheet View
An interactive, drill-down visual explorer of browsing data, plus a spreadsheet-style view for comparing site usage across custom time ranges and usage tiers.

### Media & Document Analysis (optional)
- **YouTube analysis**: videos watched, watch time, channels ranked by time spent.
- **Document analysis**: time spent on PDFs and other browser-opened documents.
- **Discord analysis**: activity view for Discord usage.
- **AI Prompt Analytics**: detects and organizes prompts submitted on supported AI platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Meta AI, Grok).

### Productivity Controls
- **Website blocking** with a configurable blocklist and blocking overlay.
- **Time limits** per site (daily, weekly, or monthly quotas).
- **Content filtering** to hide YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.
- **Flows**: group websites into custom categories like Work, Study, Research, or Communication for organized reporting.

### Reports & Notifications
Weekly browsing reports, Flow-based summaries, top-site milestone alerts, and optional AI-activity notifications.

### Account & Sync (optional)
Local-first by design. Optional sign-in and cloud sync let users keep their data available across devices, while everything stays local for those who prefer not to sync.

## Privacy Model

Resight is local-first: activity data stays on the device unless the user explicitly enables cloud synchronization. All tracking, analysis, and productivity features can be reviewed and controlled from the in-extension Settings and Privacy pages.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Resight?**
Resight is a Chrome extension that tracks how much time you spend on websites, shows historical browsing insights, and gives you tools like site blocking and time limits to stay focused.

**Is Resight a screen time tracker for Chrome?**
Yes. Resight measures daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly time spent per website, and shows trend charts, heatmaps, and hour-by-hour breakdowns of browsing activity.

**Can Resight block distracting websites?**
Yes. Users can add sites to a blocklist, set daily, weekly, or monthly time limits per site, and hide short-form content like YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

**Does Resight track AI chatbot usage like ChatGPT or Claude?**
Yes. Resight's optional AI Prompt Analytics feature detects and organizes prompts submitted on supported platforms, including ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Meta AI, and Grok.

**Does Resight store my browsing data in the cloud?**
No, not by default. Resight is local-first, meaning activity data stays on the device unless the user explicitly turns on optional cloud sync.

**Is Resight free or does it require an account?**
Resight does not require an account for local tracking, blocking, or insights. An account is only needed if the user opts into cloud sync.
